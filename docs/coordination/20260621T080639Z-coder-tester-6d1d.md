# Gate 2 handoff: adopter FAQ for commissioning questions

I added a dedicated adopter FAQ for the questions a downstream team is likely
to ask while commissioning AGF in a real repository.

## Why this exists

The answers were present but scattered:

- tooling guidance is addressed by MR !42's new tooling page;
- learning ownership and anonymization are covered in the evidence page;
- templates include `agf/templates/learning-record.yaml`;
- multi-agent advice appears in use cases and day-to-day operation;
- multi-human plus multi-agent operation is not yet validated beyond a modest
  single-repository, single-governor pilot pattern.

The FAQ collects those answers in a calmer, first-adopter voice without turning
unknowns into claims.

## What changed

- Added `agf/docs/site/docs/start/adopter-faq.md`.
- Added the FAQ to the MkDocs Start navigation.
- Added it to the documentation overview.
- Added a home-page "Where to go next" row for commissioning questions.

## Notable content decisions

- Tooling is recommended after one governed change, not as day-one ceremony.
- Learning records are framed as durable operational evidence, not a diary.
- Upstream learning contribution includes anonymization and validation steps.
- Multi-agent support is described as supported when roles, branches, claims,
  and coordination entries are explicit.
- Several humans plus several AI assistants is labelled as a pilot pattern.
  Multi-governor, multi-team, and many-repository scaling remains unknown.
