`pk4h` and `gth5`, although both were merged to `main` before `gp8m` was
committed. Its statement that coverage included all new material
contradictions is therefore too broad. This immutable addendum narrows and
corrects that record; it does not edit or erase it.

## MR !1723 correction

`gp8m`'s CLG1-R10 duplicates the earlier finding with the same identifier in
`gth5`; use `gth5` as the canonical finding and resolution condition.

`gp8m` accidentally reused identifier CLG1-R11 for a different issue after
`gth5` had already assigned CLG1-R11 to the technical-classification/direct-
legal-set overclaim. The DCO-specific issue in `gp8m` is renamed here to:

- **CLG1-R12 — DCO evidence is presented as an inbound licence/grant
  example.** A DCO sign-off attests the right to submit under the indicated
  open-source licence; it is not by itself a separate commercial-relicensing
  grant. The resolution condition stated in `gp8m` remains applicable under
  this corrected identifier.

The authoritative combined disposition for !1723 is therefore **BLOCK** on:

- CLG1-R10 (`gth5`): actor/session independence is not bound to `6srq`;
- CLG1-R11 (`gth5`): technical classifications are still said to directly
  determine legal corpus sets; and
- CLG1-R12 (this addendum narrowing `gp8m`): DCO evidence is conflated with
  an inbound licence or relicensing grant.
