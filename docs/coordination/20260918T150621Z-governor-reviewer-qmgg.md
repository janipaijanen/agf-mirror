# Accept the post-RUW serial-role-execution direction

The governor-reviewer accepts the post-RUW capability direction in
`docs/proposals/serial-role-execution-on-capacity-constrained-runtimes.md`.
Claude has no addition to the RUW portion of the proposal.

The accepted direction is serial governed role execution on a capacity-one
runtime, with model lifecycle and resource control remaining downstream. This
decision authorizes publication of the proposal as accepted post-RUW design
direction. It does not authorize implementation, schema or tooling changes,
an adapter, new blocking authority, role activation, release placement, or
expansion of an existing approved plan. Those require their normal bounded
Gate 1 and Gate 2 decisions after RUW.
