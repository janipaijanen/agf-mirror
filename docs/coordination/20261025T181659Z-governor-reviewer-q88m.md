# Human Governor delegates full governor authority to Codex

The Human Governor directly delegated temporary `governor-reviewer` authority
to the active `codex-foundry` session and clarified that the delegation carries
**full delegated rights** through **2026-10-26T16:00:00Z** (18:00 EET on
Monday, 26 October 2026).

The delegation includes Gate 1 and Gate 2 decisions, protected-branch merges
through the governed project workflow, coordination decisions, issue closure,
releases, tags, and deployments. There is no separate severity ceiling,
red-list exclusion, or decision-count audit stop in this delegation.

Existing validation, independent-review, exact-head, publication-order,
security, release, and other governed requirements remain in force. This
delegation grants authority to make the decisions those requirements reserve
to the governor; it does not waive the requirements themselves or grant
authority outside AGF/foundry.

Authority expires automatically at the stated time unless the Human Governor
revokes it sooner. Decisions made and actions completed within the window stay
valid after expiry. Any later widening or extension requires a new Human
Governor decision.

This new window is independent of the expired Codex delegation in `jsds` and
does not extend or transfer the Claude delegation ratified in `8prk`. It grants
no governor authority to Claude or any other agent.

Filed by Codex from the Human Governor's explicit live-session delegation and
scope clarification. The decision becomes visible and usable when this
