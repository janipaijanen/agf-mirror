## Proposed disposition

1. Do not sequence !654 before !2038. Dissolve the recorded collision. The
   current rule-projection contract already assigns normative ownership to
   `agf/rules/**` and treats AI loaders as projections. !654's July plan is an
   explicitly incomplete fifth-pass redesign, its inventory predates later
   rule additions, and its central mixed symlink/real-file shape remains
   portability-sensitive. Preserve it as prior analysis, then close it as
   stale/superseded after recording the replacement disposition.
2. Run one bounded current-state loader audit before proposing replacement
   work: compare the canonical rule inventory, each supported loader's actual
   coverage, and the existing projection validator. Open a new narrowly scoped
   plan only for objective gaps the audit still finds. Do not carry forward a
   repository-wide symlink conversion as the assumed solution.
3. Preserve !2038's problem statement but reject its current deletion policy.
   Replace it with a report-first maintenance plan. A cheap local count may
   trigger a full inventory; the full forge/API sweep runs on demand or at an
   explicit threshold/cadence, not as unconditional cold-start work.
4. The replacement may automatically remove only mechanically safe residue:
   stale worktree administrative entries and clean local branches that the
   freshly fetched trunk ancestry proves merged, using non-force deletion.
   Squash-merged, closed, aged/no-MR, missing-remote, dirty, or otherwise
   non-ancestral branches are review candidates. Any `git branch -D`, forced
   worktree removal, or deletion of a ref holding unique commits requires an
   explicit operator decision plus preservation evidence.
5. Once the current plans are preserved and replacement ownership is visible,
   close !654 and !2038. Planner-architect should author or amend the bounded
   Gate 1 plan; coder-tester/contribution-auditor can independently test its
   classifications and fixtures. The governor then decides Gate 1 on the new
   exact plan.

## Findings

### CT-654-1 — current plan is known incomplete and stale

Outcome: fail

Requirement strength: mandatory

Impact: material

Enforcement: blocking

Basis: `20260726T233251Z-planner-architect-ehmd` explicitly says the plan was
mid-edit, still contained contradictory sections and stale counts, and must
not be re-requested for Gate 1. The branch remains `pending-gate1`; its current
head still describes a fifth-pass July inventory while current `main` has 53
canonical rule Markdown files and 48 real `.claude/rules` files.

Resolution condition: retain the useful historical inventory, close the stale
MR, and base any successor on a scripted current-state audit.

### CT-654-2 — symlink topology is a solution assumption, not the requirement

Outcome: fail

Requirement strength: mandatory

Impact: material

Enforcement: blocking

Basis: the current canonical projection rule requires one normative owner and
discoverable loader projections; it does not require byte identity or
symlinks. The prior independent review also left portability and
template-versus-live-instance boundaries unresolved.

Resolution condition: state loader coverage and drift detection as acceptance
criteria, then choose a cross-platform projection mechanism only after the
current audit.

### CT-2038-1 — MR state and age do not prove deletion safety

Outcome: fail

Requirement strength: mandatory

Impact: critical

Enforcement: blocking

Basis: !2038 permits force deletion for a merely closed MR and for a local-only
branch with no MR after an age threshold. A closed MR may contain intentionally
retained or not-yet-preserved work. A local-only branch is, by definition, not
recoverable from hosted state. This contradicts the rule's blanket claim that
local deletion is always recoverable from the hosting platform.

Resolution condition: convert these classes to report/review candidates and
require explicit authorization and preservation evidence before force deletion.

### CT-2038-2 — squash merge does not prove every branch commit is preserved

Outcome: fail

Requirement strength: mandatory

Impact: material

Enforcement: blocking

Basis: a merged MR proves an integrated result, but a squash merge does not
prove every commit or every branch-only artifact remains reachable. `-D` is
therefore not a mechanically safe automatic action based on MR state alone.

Resolution condition: keep non-ancestral branches in the report path unless a
separate content-preservation check and operator decision authorize deletion.

### Advisory — retain the cheap trigger, reduce routine footprint

!2038 already distinguishes a cheap cold-start count from the full API sweep.
Keep that good boundary, make the trigger deterministic, and avoid prose that
causes every assistant session to repeat a costly estate scan. A thresholded
local signal plus one named maintenance owner is sufficient to surface the
work without multiplying token and forge traffic.
