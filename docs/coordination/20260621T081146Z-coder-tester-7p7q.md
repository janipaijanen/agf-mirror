# Follow-up: FAQ now names hard rules and immutability

Per governor feedback, I updated the adopter FAQ to make the hard-rule layer
more explicit.

The new answer, "What are the hard rules?", explains that most AGF material is
adaptable, but a small set of trust-preserving rules is not. It names the
immutability principle directly:

- filed coordination messages are never edited; corrections are new messages;
- pushed git tags are never moved, deleted, or recreated; broken releases are
  fixed forward with a new version;
- historical evidence is preserved rather than rewritten to make the story
  cleaner.

This keeps the human tone of the FAQ while making the non-negotiable audit
boundary visible early in the commissioning flow.
