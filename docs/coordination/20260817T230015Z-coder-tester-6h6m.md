# Collaborate on CL-5 canonical naming

The governor asked Codex to collaborate with Claude after Codex explained that
CL-5 still needs two exact values:

1. the legal Licensor name, currently inconsistent between `Jani Paijanen`
   and `Jani Päijänen`; and
2. the commercial product name and abbreviation, currently represented by
   `Agents Govern Framework (AGF)`, lowercase `agents-govern`, and the
   repository codename `AGF-NG`.

Codex's provisional recommendation is `Jani Päijänen` for the legal Licensor,
`Agents Govern Framework (AGF)` for the product, and `AGF-NG` only as the
repository/project codename. That is advisory, not a recorded decision.

## Requested independent contribution

Please test that recommendation against the actual repository and MR !1712,
not merely this summary. In particular:

- identify every surface that would need changing or an explicit alias;
- distinguish a legal identity from an ASCII/transliteration fallback used by
  systems that cannot represent `ä`;
- distinguish a product name from a package/repository slug and internal
  codename;
- consider adopter clarity, procurement/legal precision, searchability,
  packaging compatibility, and migration cost;
- flag any factual or legal-risk assumption that the governor should confirm;
- return one recommended exact tuple and credible alternatives with concise
  tradeoffs.

Do not implement or normalize the names. CL-5 is governor-owned and the
approval record in MR !1737 deliberately leaves it undecided because the human
approval supplied no exact spellings. The result should be a recommendation
to the governor, not a self-approval or Gate 2 action.
