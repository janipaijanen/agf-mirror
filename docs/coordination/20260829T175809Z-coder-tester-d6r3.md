# Plan the Memanto-derived custody and portability radar candidate

The governor asked coder-tester to file the supplied essay, “Your Agents Are
Generating Your Most Valuable IP. But do you really own it?”, as a radar
candidate. The essay identifies a useful systems question but closes with a
Memanto product pitch and collapses several distinct concepts into
“ownership.”

This cannot use the sources/radar research-maintenance fast path. That path
explicitly disqualifies legal, compliance, privacy, and security material;
the proposed candidate necessarily distinguishes contractual allocation,
copyrightability, trade-secret protection, operational custody and
portability, and provider data governance. Uncertainty therefore returns the
change to ordinary Gate 1. No radar or source file has been edited.

## Proposed smallest complete scope

Prepare a plan limited to `docs/sources.md` and `docs/radar.md` unless review
demonstrates that even this classification needs broader scope. The likely
source set is:

- Memanto product page: <https://www.memanto.ai/> (vendor self-report,
  accessed 2026-08-29);
- Memanto repository pinned at release `v0.2.18`, commit
  `3bfde8e4eacea1a78b028f7f672ac285afc57b59` (MIT-licensed implementation
  evidence, accessed 2026-08-29);
- Anthropic Commercial Terms: <https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms>
  (provider contract terms, accessed 2026-08-29);
- US Copyright Office AI copyrightability report announcement:
  <https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html> (US authority, accessed
  2026-08-29);
- EU Directive 2016/943:
  <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2016/943/oj/eng> (EU primary legal text,
  accessed 2026-08-29).

The user-supplied essay had no stable public URL. Treat it as vendor advocacy,
not durable evidence; the public product page and pinned repository can support
only their own observable or self-reported claims.

The radar question should ask whether downstream AGF guidance needs a bounded
agent-generated-state custody and portability profile: designated system of
record, provenance/licence, human review, sensitivity, retention/deletion,
export, restore, migration, and vendor-exit checks. It must also ask whether
that belongs outside AGF because AGF governs repository evidence rather than
runtime conversation memory or embedding stores.

## Required boundaries

- Preserve repository records and human gate decisions as canonical.
- Treat any search, vector, briefing, or memory layer as derived and
  non-authoritative unless a future separately governed decision says
  otherwise.
- Distinguish contractual output allocation, copyrightability,
  confidentiality/trade-secret protection, operational custody, and data
  governance; do not claim that one proves the others.
- Record Memanto maturity and performance claims as vendor self-report unless
  independently reproduced.
- Make no adoption, installation, integration, dependency, priority, issue,
  release, or implementation commitment.
- Keep the candidate explicitly controversial, non-binding, source-linked,
  and review-dated.

## Suggested acceptance shape

1. A valid Gate 1 plan names the exact source and radar edits, exclusions,
   evidence classes, legal-review limitation, and normal Gate 2 path.
2. Contribution-auditor and infosec-sentinel consultation statuses are
   recorded before governor review.
3. The eventual source entry separates observable implementation evidence
   from vendor claims and records the missing stable essay URL.
4. The eventual radar entry preserves AGF's runtime-memory boundary and frames
   a question rather than adopting Memanto or asserting legal ownership.
5. Targeted validation, fresh collision evidence, an exact-head pipeline, and
   a binding governor-reviewer Gate 2 decision precede merge.
