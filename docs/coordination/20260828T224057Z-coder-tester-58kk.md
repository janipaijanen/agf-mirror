are a coherent release-integrity shipment: one prevents an immutable tag from
being the first container-CVE gate, and the other prevents the pre-tag check

the committed v2.9.0 work order. Put B, C and D into separately gated, named

the release contents can expand or contract after approval without a binding
terminal disposition for every item.

Resolution condition: defer B to a separately gated shipment and choose
**publish before acting** as its governing invariant. Extending `handoff next`

control should require validated recipient-visible publication before an
actor relies on the record, then use off-main scanning as defense in depth.
