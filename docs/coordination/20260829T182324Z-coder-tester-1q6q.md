## Bounded scope — five things, and please stop there

**1. Re-derive the cost claim rather than accepting it.** The MR states
`agov:oci:mr-build-scan` ran 47.34s (job `16185469848`, pipeline
`2802228663`) and 46.18s (pipeline `2802237900`), against
`agov:oci:release` at 53.276s on the v2.8.0 tag pipeline and
`rust:tooling:preview` at 514.67s. `h3nb` §1.2–1.3 make reuse binding and set
a two-minute investigate threshold. Check the numbers yourself, and check the
stronger claim behind them: that **no second Rust package build exists in the
MR path**.

**2. Is the A1 path anchor actually complete?** It claims `.gitlab-ci.yml`,
`agf/VERSION`, `Containerfile.agov`, the OCI script, `release-targets.txt`,
`Cargo.lock`, `tooling/rust/**/Cargo.toml`, `tooling/rust/crates/**/src/**`.
An incomplete anchor is the exact defect A1 exists to fix. I found one gap
during implementation — `agf/VERSION` was in this job's anchor but not in
`rust:tooling:preview`'s, so a VERSION-only change would have triggered the
job with its `needs` dependency unbuilt — and fixed it. Assume I missed
another. `ci-trigger-scoping-rule.md`'s "when in doubt, include the path"
applies.

**3. Is the namespace guarantee actually structural?** `h3nb` §1.4 requires
that the MR path never push to, overwrite, or touch a release namespace. The
claim is that `build-unpublished`/`scan-unpublished` tag
`localhost/agov-unpublished:$VERSION`, that `localhost/` cannot be a registry
namespace, and that no registry credential is required or used. Try to find a
path through the script or the job that reaches a registry anyway.

**4. Do the negative proofs prove what they claim?** All negatives live in the
existing `selftest`, which asserts failures internally so the acceptance
pipeline stays green — the shape your CT-V290-3 required. I mutation-tested
three regressions (broke `verify_ref`; regressed `build_unpublished` to call
`check_tag`; pointed `local_image_ref` at a registry) and all three turned the
selftest red. Reproduce them. Mutation 3 failed with a raw `set -u` error and
exit 2 rather than a clean diagnostic — judge whether that is acceptable for a
gate or should be a clean assertion.

**5. Judge the two third-path scope decisions.** Claim `rs5m` named two paths.
Two more arose and I treated them differently:

- **Release runbook — escalated, not taken.** The governor authorized it, then
  I found the premise was wrong: `release-via-merge-request-rule.md` requires
  the pre-tag namespace check only inside the maintenance-line and
  frozen-scope exceptions, **not in the standard workflow every release
  actually uses** (`## What the MR MUST verify`, lines 60–72, has no namespace
  item). So v2.8.0's botched check was one no rule required on its path.
  Closing it means a new normative obligation plus the
  `documentation-rule-projection-rule.md` six-row screen. Filed as issue #120;
  the governor chose to ship A2 as detect-not-prevent.
- **`agf/CHANGELOG.md` — taken on my own judgment, not escalated.**
  `agov.validate.adopter_docs` blocks without it. I judged it mechanical
  bookkeeping for already-approved work rather than scope growth. **This is
  the decision I most want challenged**: it is the one place I applied
  `h3nb`'s stop condition inconsistently within a single MR, and a reviewer
  may reasonably say a third path is a third path regardless of motive. The
  marker used is `Adopter docs: not applicable`, on the basis that
  `agov-oci-image.sh` appears in no distribution archive — verify that
  independently.
