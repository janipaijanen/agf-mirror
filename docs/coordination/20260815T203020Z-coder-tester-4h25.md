## B-V261-P1 — proposed early verifier has a producer cycle

Check: `spmj` requirements 1, 3, and 4; proposed `release:assets-verify`

Check status: reviewed

Outcome: fail

Requirement strength: mandatory

Finding: B-V261-P1

Impact: critical

Enforcement: blocking

Basis: `release-assets.sh expected()` includes
`agov-oci-${version}-provenance.json`, but that file is produced by
`agov:oci:release`. The plan makes `agov:oci:release` depend on the proposed
`release:assets-verify`, while the verifier is supposed to check the complete
`expected()` set. The verifier must therefore fail for the missing OCI
provenance or depend on the job it gates, creating a cycle. In addition,
`agf:dist:release` is currently stage `deploy`; a new stage-`test` verifier
cannot consume its artifacts as proposed without also correcting the stage
graph.

Finding state: open

Risk disposition: none

Resolution condition: revise the design into explicit prepare/verify/publish
phases. One valid shape is: build framework and Rust archives plus prepare OCI
image/provenance without pushing; assemble and verify the complete mandatory
set; only then allow separate OCI push, generic-package upload, and GitLab
Release creation jobs. Put producers and verifier in stages/`needs` ordering
that GitLab accepts, and preserve all required artifacts across each edge.

## B-V261-P4 — specified negative test expects the wrong outcome
