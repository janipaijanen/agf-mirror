# Ratify `khhr` for Codex approval of Claude-authored work

Recorded by Codex as the durable transcription of the Human Governor's binding
decision in the current live session; this is not an AI-originated authority
grant.

After publication and independent review of bridge record
`20261026T171229Z-improvement-propeller-khhr` on MR !3250 at exact head
`477737be30a914dd53a7a2113661d259450841ee`, the Human Governor stated:

> khhr approved.

The Human Governor thereby ratifies the prospective part of `khhr` that
authorizes Codex, until `2026-10-27T18:00:00Z`, to decide Gate 1, decide Gate 2,
and merge Claude-authored non-red-list work. Every use remains subject to all
conditions and exclusions in `khhr`, including:

- an independent `CLEAR` at the exact current head;
- completed required consultations by someone other than the author;
- a green required exact-head pipeline;
- a decision record published before merge or another irreversible action;
- invalidation of the `CLEAR` by any new head; and
- return to the Human Governor for `OTHER`, disagreement, unresolved blockers,
  ambiguous or mixed authorship, or an excluded category.

Codex may not approve or merge Codex-authored work under this decision. Releases,
tags, deploys, rule changes, role-definition changes, schema changes,
security-boundary changes, delegation changes, and every other currently
never-delegable category remain excluded.

This decision does **not** ratify the reciprocal Claude-to-Codex authority
recorded in `qqg3`. It also does not ratify `75qt`, `q65r`, `gnqr`, `86ps`, the
already-executed merges of MRs !3245 or !3244, or any other earlier action.
Those matters require separate Human Governor decisions that name them
explicitly.

This record authorizes no merge by itself; each eligible item still requires
its own exact-head decision record under the conditions above.
