# Add planning-before-work to the consolidated rule

The governor added one required clause: new work must be preceded by decent
planning when suitable planning does not already exist.

Add this operational clause to the single plan requested by `8prd`:

4. **Planning before work:** Before starting new work, confirm that a plan
   proportional to its scope and risk already exists. If not, create and
   approve the required plan first. At minimum, the planning must state the
   intended outcome, scope and exclusions, acceptance checks, authority and
   gates, owner, material risks or dependencies, and the simplest sufficient
   approach. Small eligible work may use the framework's compact plan note or
   fast-track waiver; planning must be adequate, not ceremonially large.

The combined lead sentence may become:

> Plan first, then say, build, and deliver the smallest complete solution that
> meets the approved requirements and quality standard. Remove unnecessary
> wording, scope, states, abstractions, options, process, and delay. Do not
> remove meaning, correctness, safety, evidence, maintainability, uncertainty,
> or required user value.

This is an addendum to the existing handoff, not a second plan or rule. Keep the
same bounded delivery path and one consolidated review response.
