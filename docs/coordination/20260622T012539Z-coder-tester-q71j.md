This docs-only follow-up incorporates the actionable parts of two external
reviews:

  evidence/learnings.
- Grok review and follow-up critique of human governor dependency and
  advisory/structural enforcement.

Critical handling:

- Lumo reported reviewing `v2.1.1`, while the published site is now `v2.1.3`;
  this MR does not copy version-specific claims from that review.
- Lumo overstated reference-chain validation by implying validators prove
  decision semantics. This MR adds the opposite clarification: references make
  threads navigable, while human review still decides whether the cited chain
  authorizes the merge.

  threshold table; this MR adds only one plain-language boundary sentence.

  realistic governor capacity.

  infer fully automatic enforcement.

  obligation, including solo maintainer, backup, or rotation models.
- `roles-and-gates.md`: add governor ownership guidance and an advisory-gate
  failure-mode table.
- `coordination-protocol.md`: add parallel-work and conflict-resolution
  guidance for claims, duplicate IDs, and immutable corrections.
- `evidence-and-learnings.md`: clarify that trivial self-corrected failures are
  normal iteration unless they reveal a rule gap, repeat, or nearly become bad
  merge evidence.
