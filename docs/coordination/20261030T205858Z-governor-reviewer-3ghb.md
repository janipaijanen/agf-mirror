## B. Small task-metrics amendment

Accept 7gsk's simplification: amend the existing Pages plan with one small table
and a short measurement note, referencing its existing controls rather than
repeating them. Keep Decide / Get started / Work with AGF labels and the six
implementation slices. Map Evaluate / Adopt / Operate to those sections.

For each job give a stated scenario, Home start, checkable end and answer key,
measured before values and labelled after targets. Use link transitions as the
counted route metric (target at most three for the named task); note mobile
menu/search friction separately. Also retain required-reading word count and
avoidable reader-choice count as advisory before/after measures: the human's
explicit aim is less required reading, which fewer links alone cannot prove.
Do not count genuine governance gates as choices to eliminate. Do not fabricate
values; record uncertainty and whether each result comes from rendered expert
inspection or actual participants.

Evaluate should support adopt/defer/not-fit with obligations understood. Adopt
must identify the concrete next setup action for a stated scenario, with an
exact file/command only when required by that scenario: optional CLI/MCP must
not become prerequisites. Operate should identify the correct ordinary-change
step, gate and required artifact/message from a clear scenario and answer key.
These are task/answerability endpoints, not proof of completed installation or
an approved/merged software change. Refine endpoints explicitly if they do not
serve the human's intended task.

The five-minute figure is not acceptance evidence without unfamiliar-user
measurement. Defer it as an unvalidated future usability target, not a public
performance claim. Preserve the original independent-review and expert-
provenance limits. This amendment must receive its required consultations and
binding Gate 1 before changed criteria govern implementation.

Carry the two minor proposal wording fixes from m37b into the next necessary
review-artifact revision on the relevant planning branch; do not launch a
separate pipeline for those edits alone. Keep the two planning deltas bounded
and independently reviewable. No site, canonical owner, rule, role, template,
