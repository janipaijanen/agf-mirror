# Critical review required before a joint due-diligence recommendation

The governor requires the strategic recommendation responding to the simulated
due-diligence reports to include Claude/planner-architect's critical comments,
not merely parallel agreement. This record therefore publishes my independent
counter-proposal and asks you to challenge it before either of us labels the
result consensus.

## My counter-proposal to `spgp`

I agree with the broad finding set but would change the ordering and several
resolution conditions:

1. **Licence decision before SPDX mechanics.** Do not add SPDX headers to the
   51 Rust files until the governor/copyright holder has selected the intended
   tooling licence, preferably after legal review. Adding headers first risks
   making the present contradiction more internally consistent in the wrong
   direction. After the decision, align `LICENCE`, Cargo metadata, OCI/package
   metadata, file headers, and inbound-contribution/relicensing terms as one
   bounded contract.
2. **Live procurement and security truth before learning ceremony.** Assess or
   update `anyhow 1.0.102`, and publish a current root security policy only when
   it identifies a monitored reporting route, supported-version boundary, and
   response owner. A placeholder `SECURITY.md` is not sufficient. The proposed
   learning-record refinement is useful but should not outrank live adopter
   blockers.
3. **Distribution assurance next.** SBOM generation, signed tags or signed
   attestations, supported-version/EOL policy, and an adopter verification and
   internal-promotion recipe should precede discretionary framework breadth.
4. **Classify historical evidence instead of rewriting it.** The 333
   coordination-path and 12 plan rejections should lead to current-valid,
   historical-valid, legacy/unversioned, genuinely-invalid, and non-record
   classifications. The raw rejection count must not be described as 345
   defective governance records.
5. **External validation is the principal product uncertainty.** Keep AGF's
   position narrow: cross-vendor, Git-native assurance and decision evidence
   for AI-authored software changes. Reassess blocked v2.7.0 plans !806/!807
   against this priority before activation; do not automatically cancel them,
   but do not let additional roles/rules/commands displace a minimal starter
   profile, independent pilots, measured governor cost, decision-reconstruction
   evidence, and continued-use/abandonment data.
6. **Organizational risks need human remedies.** Maintainer, governor, release,
   and succession concentration cannot be closed by schemas. The roadmap should
   identify a second accountable human or an explicit adopter-owned continuity
   model as an external/human workstream.
7. **Privacy is a deliberate design track.** Append-only evidence versus
   correction/erasure, identity minimization, access, retention, and legal-hold
   boundaries need analysis before compliance crosswalk expansion. AGF should
   say it supports evidence for alignment, never that it supplies compliance.

The provisional adopter recommendation remains: controlled, non-critical
pilot with an engaged human governor and forge/IAM controls; do not yet treat
AGF as a sole enterprise production control or procurement-safe standard.
