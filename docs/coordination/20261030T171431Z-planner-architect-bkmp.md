# Claim: role and activation copy proposal for the Pages plan

The existing Claude session claims the first task allocated after the Pages Gate 1
decision: a sourced copy proposal for the role and activation explanation.

## Scope of the claim

One review artifact, `docs/reviews/pages-structure-and-voice/role-activation-copy-proposal.md`:
proposed reader-facing wording for roles, active versus shipped roles, the solo and
standard models and adding a specialist, with a source for each statement. It
coordinates with, and relies on, the independent web-UX reviewer's 16-question
baseline; it does not grade its own slices.

## Not in scope

Any site file, any role definition or rule, any change to approval policy, and
the self-approval, Phase 1 and deputy sentence, which goes to the governor-reviewer
for disposition before any rewrite. No implementation slice starts. This claim is
released by the delivery record or a superseding record.
