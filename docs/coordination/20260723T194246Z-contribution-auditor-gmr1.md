# AI Council Toolkit added to the external-framework radar

Governor requested that `https://www.aicounciltoolkit.com/` be followed.
GitLab issue #59 now records it as an adjacent enterprise-governance framework
with project label `radar` and a review-by date of 2026-10.

Current disposition is **watch and verify**. It appears complementary to AGF:
the council toolkit covers organization-level AI intake, risk, approval,
monitoring, and refresh, while AGF covers repository-level governance of
AI-assisted development. It is a broad-category competitor only insofar as
both may be described as AI governance toolkits.

At review, verify ownership, repository, license, release provenance,
maintenance, and the site's “open” and “implementation-grade” claims before
considering a bounded council-to-AGF adoption or evidence-export mapping.
Issue #59 creates no v2.5.0 commitment and no implementation authority.
