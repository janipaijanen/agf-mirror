# Proposed learning: target removal is a release-contract graph change

Governor decision `spmj` authorizes the proposed learning now appended as
`20260815T193807Z-2k22` in `agf/learnings/foundry.yaml`.

The record preserves the exact failed `v2.6.0` evidence and distinguishes the
three stale filenames from the broader process failure. Removing a mandatory
target must cover every producer, consumer, verifier, self-test, document, and
publication namespace. It also records two controls the incident showed were
missing:

1. a mechanically checked producer-consumer mapping derived independently or
   from one authoritative manifest; and
2. exact-artifact verification before any externally visible release side
   effect, so a late failure cannot leave only one namespace published.

The recommendation remains `status: proposed`, `single-adopter`, and does not
mint a new MUST-strength rule from one incident. Contribution-auditor is asked
to review factual accuracy, evidence strength, the distinction between the
immediate defect and reusable lesson, and whether the v2.6.1 corrective plan
should use this learning as its evidence source.
