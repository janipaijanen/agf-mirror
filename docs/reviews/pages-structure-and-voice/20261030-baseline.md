## Summary

- **PASS:** 12
- **AMBIGUOUS:** 3 — questions 3, 7, and 10
- **FAIL:** 1 — question 15

The clearest current gap is the missing actionable specialist request and activation path. The most authority-sensitive ambiguity is the unreconciled public human-only approval wording versus the shipped deputy-governor rule and Phase 1 conflict guidance.

## Solo scenario

**Result: PASS for the documented common case, with a policy caveat.**

Route: Home → Decide → Roles and gates / Adopter FAQ.

The current public pages present a solo maintainer using one assistant sequentially for planning and implementation while the human maintainer acts as governor and records both gate decisions. They also present gate approval as remaining human. This baseline records that presentation; it does not resolve the separate person-role conflict or bounded deputy-delegation tension identified in question 3.

## Active, inactive, and new-specialist scenarios

- **Consult an already-active specialist:** Partially inferable. The site explains that specialist findings are advisory and do not supply gate authority, but it does not provide a concise task path for requesting and handing off that consultation.
- **Activate an inactive shipped specialist:** **FAIL.** The current site does not show the complete authorized assignment, player/scope, live-roster, and handoff sequence.
- **Design a new specialist role:** Partially documented under **Customizing the framework → Adding roles**, but the governed evolution/approval sequence and its distinction from activation are not presented as an actionable route.

These findings support the approved plan's first roles/activation slice and its requirement to resolve authority wording against canonical owners before later prose depends on it.
