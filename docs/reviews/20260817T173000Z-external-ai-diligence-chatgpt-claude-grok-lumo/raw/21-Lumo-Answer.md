Bottom Line
Seven of ten identified risks are project-specific and relate to AGF's current maturity stage (sole maintainer, no security policy, unsigned releases, no independent adoption, ShareAlike license, no funding, no community). These could be addressed if the project attracts resources and attention.
Three risks reflect broader industry realities:

Lack of regulatory certification (market-wide)
Competition from platform-native solutions (structural market trend)
Toolchain/security layering requirements (universal for AI governance)

The recommendation to proceed only for experimentation/PoC reflects the project-specific risks. Even if AGF were to achieve all its remediation goals, the broader industry risks would persist and would need enterprise-level responses regardless of the tool selected.
