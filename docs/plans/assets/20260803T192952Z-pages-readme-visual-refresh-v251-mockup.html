What AGF gives you

Six controls, added to a repo you already have.

Each bullet below is one independent claim — no compound sentences carrying two ideas at once.

01 · roles
A named human governor approves the plan before implementation starts (Gate 1) and approves the tested change before it merges (Gate 2).
02 · authority
Proposing, implementing, reviewing, and approving are separate, named responsibilities.
03 · records
Plans, handoffs, reviews, and decisions live as repository files tied to exact revisions.
04 · evidence
Every public capability claim states whether it's planned, implemented, validated, or deployable — and how well-evidenced that status is.
05 · learning
Operational evidence can become a reviewed learning record; nothing changes rules or scope automatically.
06 · exit
No runtime or service holds your data — pause, downgrade, or remove it whenever you want.
