## What this plan is, and is not

**It is:** a bounded IN / CANDIDATE / DEFER disposition of the post-v2.10
workstreams into v2.11, their ordering and dependencies, and the release
cut. It is derived from and must stay consistent with the governed roadmap
owner `docs/roadmap.md` (merged in queue-convergence B1) and the collision
disposition `ds4k`.

**It is not:** a re-approval of any workstream's own Gate 1 or Gate 2, a
version bump, a schema/validator/CI/rule change, or an implementation work
order. Each admitted workstream keeps its own Gate 1, exact-head reviews,
and binding Gate 2. This plan sets the scope boundary and sequence only.

1. every **merge-based** IN workstream has merged at its own Gate 2; and
2. every **non-merge acceptance condition** of an IN workstream is
   complete. For queue convergence, every terminal disposition represented
   by an IN-scope B2/B3/B4/B5/B6/B7/B8 carrier or ledger must have been
   applied and verified after its merge: source MRs are actually closed where
   closure is the disposition, and park/reconstruct outcomes are recorded and

   genuine first unattended Propeller wake **and** its separate Level-5
   follow-up record; P-1 (`3jh6`) is attended Level-4 evidence and does not

   evidence/coordination events, not merges; "all MRs merged" does not imply

CP-B is explicitly part of condition 1: its implementation must be merged and
its publication-integrity evidence complete before the release-metadata MR may
open. There is no fallback that cuts v2.11 without CP-B; removing or carrying
it to v2.12 requires a new governor scope decision superseding row 7.

## Release cut — delegated to a mandatory child plan

This scope plan **does not** contain an executable release-cut procedure
and **grants no tag, release, or publication authority**. Cutting v2.11
requires two things this plan does not supply:

1. **A child v2.11 release-publication plan** with its own Gate 1. It — not
   this plan — specifies completely: the exact metadata allowlist; the
   current-version inventory across every governed surface; source/target
   drift invalidation; retention of the preflighted local-only metadata
   commit as the MR head; runner/DinD readiness checks; the protected-tag
   policy checkpoints (below); and forward-only recovery after the tag is
   pushed. The child plan owns the `agov dist` / `agov release` command
   sequence with **actual verified job names**, not the release skill's
   stale `build-dist -> create-release` shorthand.
2. **A separate binding governor decision** authorizing the tag and
   publication, made *after* the v2.11 release-metadata MR has merged. A
   merged metadata MR is not itself tag/publication authorization
   (foundry `AGENTS.md`; v2.10 precedent `2kgq`).

  changelogs (Improvement Propeller **activation and evidenced Level 5** entry
  — `Changed`, adopters opt in per the rule Scope, with the genuine wake and

  publication-efficiency entries delivered by rows 5–7; queue-

  `framework_version` bumps; and the `docs/roadmap.md` +
  `agf/docs/site/roadmap.md` sequence-table sync (see "Roadmap-owner

## Risks and controls

- **Scope balloon.** The coordination cluster + `qbka` + #129 could each
  grow. Control: this plan admits *membership and sequence* only; each
  workstream's own Gate 1 bounds its size, and CANDIDATE items slip to
  v2.12 rather than expanding v2.11. Governor decision `er78` knowingly
  enlarged the IN surface from five to seven rows; every IN row remains a
  release-cut precondition unless a later governor decision explicitly
  supersedes its membership.
- **The #129 → `qbka` freeze dependency slips**, blocking #122 and the
  plan-MR cohort. Control: the #129 slice is deliberately the *smallest*
  freeze-blocking unit (per `6s5d`), not the full assessment.
- **Propeller Level 5 does not land in the window.** Control: because #131 is
  IN and its genuine wake + separate Level-5 record are explicit non-merge
  release-cut preconditions, v2.11 does not cut. Shipping it as merely
  activated / Level-5-pending requires a new governor scope decision that
  supersedes row 2 and the release-cut condition; changelog wording cannot
  silently weaken the scope (`qkj2`). #132 remains blocked on P-3 regardless.
- **B4's archive preserves records but degrades discovery.** Control: !2581
  does not reach Gate 2 until the 39 archive-path visibility findings are
  removed without hiding real top-level obligations, or the governor records
  an explicit risk/scope disposition. A green pipeline alone is insufficient.
- **CP-B weakens timely coordination while trying to reduce `main` churn.**
  Control: CP-B is now IN, but its own Gate 1 must preserve an urgent bypass
  and mechanically bounded multi-record publication, and its implementation
  must include the row 7 negative gate-self-test. The release cut waits for
  that merged and evidenced implementation; a one-cut `2brd` fallback does not
  satisfy this plan.
- **Author/reviewer collapse.** Control: non-author contribution audit;
  disclosed here and in the `advisory_consultation` row.
