## Trigger

The parent plan's required v2.4.5 outcome is an auditable, current-state
critical review of the published documentation using a shared protocol
across multiple LLM perspectives. Historical model assessments (Lumo, Claude
Code, Grok, ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, OpenAI Web, Codex, GPT-5.6 Sol
High) exist but were performed at different times, against different
snapshots, and with different scopes and prompts — they are useful
provenance, not evidence that the current implementation was critically
reviewed. This plan defines the frozen protocol, including the complete
review packet, so a fresh, comparable, provenance-complete review can run
once, against one pinned baseline.

### Inaccessible pages

If a reviewer's session cannot fetch a given pinned URL (network restriction,
tool sandboxing, provider-side link-fetch failure), the reviewer records
"page N: not accessible in this session" as a finding-table row with no
fabricated content, and does not substitute a different page, infer the
page's likely content, or skip silently. The provenance record (below) must
list every page actually read and every page marked inaccessible.

## Shared review packet

`docs/reviews/v2.4.5-review-packet.md` is the **sole normative copy-paste
source** for the complete reviewer-facing packet. Every reviewer receives
that file's full rendered text verbatim and unmodified after this plan clears
Gate 1. Operators must not reconstruct the packet from this plan, concatenate
individual sections manually, or maintain a second supposedly verbatim copy.

The target-page table above defines and explains the governed scope; its
repetition inside the packet is a reader-facing projection, not a second
packet source. If the packet changes before Gate 1, the plan must be reviewed
against the changed packet. After Gate 1, changing the packet invalidates the
approval and requires re-confirmation before execution.

Reviewers submit an independent first pass before seeing any other
reviewer's findings. No reviewer may be shown another reviewer's output
before submitting their own.

## Minimum panel, independence, and vendor-diversity labeling

- **Claude** (this assistant's own review pass).
- **Codex** and **GPT-5.6 Sol High** as two distinct review units — one
  assistant surface (Codex) and one model/interface (GPT-5.6 Sol High) are
  not interchangeable and must not be counted as a single reviewer. **Both
  are OpenAI-family models accessed through different surfaces — they are
  distinct review units, but they are not independent vendors.** Agreement
  between Codex and GPT-5.6 Sol High must be labeled and treated as
  same-vendor corroboration, weighted no higher than a single vendor's
  perspective, never presented as cross-vendor agreement.
- At least **two** externally supplied independent model perspectives
  selected from providers outside the OpenAI and Anthropic families where
  practical (e.g. Gemini, Grok, or another named system), plus optionally
  Cursor, GitHub Copilot, or Lumo if those add a genuinely distinct
  underlying model rather than another wrapper over an already-represented
  vendor.
- **Minimum provider diversity for this review to be described as
  "multi-vendor" in any synthesis or public claim: at least three distinct
  model-providing organizations** (e.g. Anthropic, OpenAI, and one of
  Google/xAI/other), each contributing at least one captured review unit.
  If fewer than three distinct providers are actually captured, the
  synthesis must say so plainly ("N providers, M review units") rather than
  imply broader coverage.

Each actual review unit is enumerated separately with its own product,
interface, model, and session/exposure fields. Record the exact model
identifier when the provider exposes it; otherwise record the product,
date, interface, and the identifier limitation explicitly (do not omit the
field silently). Do not list a model as a reviewer unless its output is
actually captured. A compound label (e.g. "Codex / GPT-5.6") must not
double-count one captured output as two reviewers.

## Per-review provenance (required fields)

For every review unit's submission, record:

- reviewer/product name
- exact model identifier, or the stated limitation if the provider does not
  expose one
- underlying model-providing organization (for the vendor-diversity count
  above)
- UTC date of the review
- reviewed SHA/tag and URL (must be `v2.4.4` /
  `103930dbcdb9127cea65f1ca387ef13ed3ee7c10` for all reviewers)
- the complete prompt/protocol text sent (verbatim from the sole normative
  `docs/reviews/v2.4.5-review-packet.md` source, unmodified)
- the unedited response
- normalized findings extracted from that response, each tagged with the
  required output shape's fields, including which pages were marked
  inaccessible

## Synthesis procedure

1. Collect all review-unit submissions with full provenance.
2. Deduplicate findings that describe the same underlying defect across
   reviewers, but do not convert vote count into truth — a finding raised
   by one reviewer and missed by four others is not thereby false, and a
   finding raised by all five is not thereby proven without independent
   fact-check. Same-vendor corroboration (Codex + GPT-5.6 Sol High agreeing)
   counts as one vendor's perspective for weighting purposes, not two.
3. Separate findings into two tracks, not four — this replaces the prior
   revision's four-category split, which contradicted itself between
   "usability findings are subjective but actionable" and "usability
   findings are excluded":
   - **Track A — preserved for governor disposition.** This track contains
     (a) factually verifiable findings confirmed true against the pinned
     source by fact-checker; (b) usability/ordering judgments corroborated by
     at least two independent review units from at least two distinct vendors
     where the panel achieves that diversity; and (c) every in-scope
     usability/ordering judgment raised by only one reviewer. Category (c)
     remains visible and is labeled **non-corroborated, lower-confidence**;
     it is not discarded, silently demoted to Track B, or presented as vendor
     agreement. All Track A findings go to the human governor for disposition.
     Only confirmed category (a), corroborated category (b), or a category
     (c) finding the governor explicitly accepts may enter a separately gated,
     bounded documentation remediation.
   - **Track B — excluded from this workstream.** Roadmap or scope-expansion
     proposals (new features, schema/role/validator changes), and claims
     that would require human or empirical evidence this review cannot
     supply (e.g. "adopters find this confusing" asserted without adopter
     data) unless independently corroborated as a documentation-clarity
     issue per Track A's own test. Findings that would change schemas,
     roles, validators, authority, CI enforcement, or adopter contracts are
     always Track B regardless of corroboration, per the parent plan's
     v2.5.0 routing rule.
4. Route every factual claim and factual premise in Track A through
   fact-checker verification against the exact pinned tree before it may be
   cited as fact in any synthesis document. Usability judgments are not
   converted into factual claims and do not require fact-checker
   sign-off — they require the corroboration/confidence label from step 3 and
   honest labeling as judgment, not fact.
5. Route the evidence-strength and independence-labeling correctness of the
   whole package — including the vendor-diversity disclosure above — through
   academic-peer-reviewer before it is treated as publishable.
6. Route the final package and proposed remediation dispositions through
   contribution-auditor.
7. Produce one synthesis document listing: Track A findings (with reviewer
   count, vendor diversity, corroboration basis, and confidence label stated
   per finding, including single-reviewer observations), and
   Track B findings (explicitly excluded, with the routing destination —
   v2.5.0, backlog, or "insufficient evidence" — stated per finding).

## Retention, copyright, and privacy boundaries

- Prompts sent to external, non-project-controlled LLM providers MUST follow
  `external-anonymization-rule.md`: codename-only across the codebase
  boundary if the project's real identity is not already intentionally
  public. This project currently publishes under its real name on a public
  GitLab instance; if that remains true at execution time, this constraint
  is satisfied by the project's own public visibility and does not require
  additional redaction — record that determination explicitly in the
  synthesis document rather than assuming it silently.
- Raw external-reviewer responses are retained as committed fixtures under
  `docs/reviews/` with their full provenance fields wherever they can
  lawfully and safely be committed verbatim — a future auditor must be able
  to trace any synthesis claim back to the unedited response it came from.
  **Where a specific response cannot be committed verbatim** (provider
  terms of service prohibit redistribution, the response contains
  third-party copyrighted material, or it contains PII/secrets that cannot
  be redacted without destroying the finding's evidentiary value), the
  synthesis document records that limitation explicitly per review unit
  ("response not retained verbatim: [reason]") and retains only the
  normalized, de-identified finding rows that step 4-5 of the synthesis
  procedure actually rely on — the limitation does not block synthesis, it
  bounds what evidence is publicly re-shareable.
- No PII, credentials, or secrets may appear in any submitted prompt or
  returned response committed to the repository. Screen before commit per
  `security-hardening-rule.md`.
- External-provider terms of service regarding submitted content (training
  use, retention) are the responsibility of whoever executes each review
  session with that provider; this plan does not itself authorize accepting
  any specific provider's terms — that remains a human decision at
  execution time.

## Explicit exclusions

- This plan does not execute any review itself — execution requires Gate 1
  approval first.
- This plan does not authorize contacting or purchasing access to external
  model providers. Human-supplied outputs may be incorporated only with the
  full provenance fields above.
- This plan does not predetermine findings or their remediation.
- Findings that would change schemas, roles, validators, authority, CI
  enforcement, or adopter contracts are routed to v2.5.0 (Track B), per the
  parent plan, not remediated under this documentation-patch workstream.
