# Plan: AGF v2.4.3 — documentation-quality and information-architecture backlog

## Origin and scope boundary

v2.4.2 (`docs/plans/v2.4.2-public-experience-and-evidence-presentation.md`)
explicitly froze its own scope and excluded, without re-verifying, the 11
WARN/INFO items carried forward from the post-release audit
`20260721T212235Z-planner-architect-qj7s`. Per that plan's own words: "any
of them entering scope requires a plan amendment and new Gate 1 ... that
re-verifies its live status first." This plan is that re-verification and
Gate 1 submission, done as a **separate** release rather than folded into
v2.4.2, so v2.4.2's own patch stays a clean, narrowly-scoped yes/no for its
reviewers.

This plan does **not** cover issue #53 (CI/build-latency measurement,
explicitly deferred by `sgp8`/`8neq`) or the `seq` field deprecation
decision flagged open in `deprecation-policy-rule.md`. Both are different
risk/effort classes than a documentation-quality patch and are left for
their own separately-scoped work.

## Baseline

Re-verified against `main` at `345680a` (2026-07-24), the tip at the time
of this plan's drafting.

## Live-status re-verification of the 11 carried-forward `qj7s` items

Every item below was checked against the current repository content before
being included or excluded — none are carried forward on the strength of
the original 2026-07-21 finding alone.

### Confirmed still live — in scope

1. **Hardcoded version-pin literals with no release-time validator.**
   Confirmed 5 locations pin the literal current version
   (`registry.gitlab.com/.../agov:2.4.1`): `README.md:164`,
   `agf/docs/site/docs/adopt/tooling-and-tokens.md:85`,
   `agf/docs/site/docs/advanced/contracts-and-validators.md:113,153`,
   `agf/docs/site/docs/adopt/install-and-layouts.md:242,246`. All are
   correct *today* (matching `agf/VERSION` = `2.4.1`) — same fragility
   character as the original finding: nothing fails today, nothing catches
   drift at the next release.
2. **Misdirected metadata link.** `agf/docs/site/docs/index.md:109` and
   `agf/docs/site/docs/concepts/roles-and-gates.md:34` both link "Quick
   reference" to the legacy GitLab slug
   `gitlab.com/janipaijanen/agent-governance` instead of the canonical
   `agents-govern-framework` slug used everywhere else in the site
   (confirmed zero other occurrences of the legacy slug in the codebase,
   and the canonical slug is the one used in every release-artifact and
   registry reference checked above). This is now a **two-occurrence**
   defect, not the original finding's one.
3. **Grey overload / off-palette color.** `agf/docs/figures/src/
   governance-loop.dot` and `agf/docs/figures/src/adoption-exit-flow.dot`
   still contain the off-palette `#CCCCCC` value flagged in the original
   finding. The broader grey-semantic-overload claim (active-Learn-step,
   decommissioned-state, and advisory-status all using the same grey)
   needs a direct cross-check against `agf/docs/figures/palette.md` during
   implementation — the off-palette literal is independently confirmed
   regardless of how that broader question resolves.
4. **Level-2 maturity-vocabulary naming inconsistency.** Public docs
   (`agf/docs/site/evaluate.md:134`,
   `agf/docs/site/ai-evaluation-dossier.md:149`,
   `agf/docs/site/reference/maturity-vocabulary.md:18`, `README.md:91`)
   consistently name Level 2 "Surface-defined." The project's own
   `.claude/rules/docs/implementation-honesty-rule.md` (lines 29, 36, 66)
   names the same level "Schema-defined." Both are internally consistent
   within their own document but contradict each other across the
   public/rule boundary — confirmed still present, unchanged since
   `qj7s`.
5. **`bgcolor=white` tension with `palette.md`'s light-background-only
   assumption.** Confirmed 7 figure sources still hardcode
   `bgcolor="white"`. Per the original finding's own framing, this is a
   flagged *tension* to resolve one way or the other (either document the
   light-background assumption as accepted and intentional, or make the
   figures background-agnostic), not a one-directional "bug."
6. **No `<figure>`/`<figcaption>` semantics; alt text duplicates the
   title.** Confirmed zero `<figcaption>` occurrences sitewide, and
   sampled alt text (`![Gate pipeline](...)`,
   `![Coordination channel](...)`) is the bare figure title with no
   additional descriptive content, exactly matching the original finding.

### Confirmed resolved — excluded, with evidence

- **Competing evaluator entry points.** `evaluate.md` is now explicitly
  scoped to "external AI assistants and independent human reviewers" and
  is linked only from `ai-evaluation-dossier.md`; `index.md` routes the
  adoption decision cleanly and repeatedly to
  `docs/start/should-you-adopt.md`. The three-nav-levels-deep confusion
  the original finding described is gone.
- **README duplicates site content with volatile counts.** No hardcoded
  page/figure/diagram counts found in the current `README.md`.
- **Missing verify-your-download step.** `agf/docs/site/docs/adopt/
  install-and-layouts.md:22-26` now documents `sha256sum -c` verification
  against the published `.sha256` and `-provenance.json` files.
- **No root `CONTRIBUTING.md`.** `agents-govern/CONTRIBUTING.md` exists
  and `README.md:179` links a hosted "Contribute" page. Not a bare gap;
  no further action proposed here.

### Re-scoped or dropped — reasoning stated, not silently carried

- **Duplicate "what to read next" tables.** Reduced from the original
  three (index/docs-index/evaluate) to two: `index.md`'s "Where to go
  next" (intent → page, task-routing) and `docs/index.md`'s "The path"
  (staged learning path). These now serve genuinely different reader
  intents rather than repeating the same table, and `evaluate.md` no
  longer carries one at all. **Not included as a defect** — if a future
  reviewer disagrees, that is a fresh finding against current content, not
  a resurrection of the 2026-07-21 finding.
- **Foundry-specific filenames in adopter-facing docs.** Current instances
  (`install-and-layouts.md:205`, `coordination-protocol.md:288`,
  `tooling-and-tokens.md:150,154`) are explicitly self-labeled as "AGF
  foundry" / "AGF-NG/foundry dogfooding" examples, not presented as
  generic placeholders. This reads as the intended, disclosed use of the
  project's own dogfooding evidence, consistent with
  `external-anonymization-rule.md`'s codebase/external boundary (codenames
  are expected *inside* the codebase). **Not included** — the original
  finding predates that rule's current framing and no unlabeled instance
  was found on re-check.
- **CLI (`agov --help`) onboarding pointer.** Not independently
  re-verified in this pass; carried as an **open question** rather than
  either included or excluded. If contribution-auditor or the governor
  wants it in scope, it is a small, independently-reviewable addition and
  can be folded in before Gate 1 approval without changing this plan's
  overall shape.

### Explicitly out of scope for this patch (real gap, wrong work-type)

- **Missing macOS/darwin release build target.** Confirmed:
  `agf/docs/site/reference/release-artifacts.md` lists only
  `linux-x86_64`, `linux-aarch64`, and `windows-x86_64` Rust tooling
  archives. This is a genuine gap, but it is a **release-engineering /
  CI addition** (new cross-compilation target, signing if applicable,
  CI job), not a documentation fix — the site's current wording already
  states the platform list accurately rather than misrepresenting it. Do
  not fold a build-target addition into a doc-quality patch; if wanted,
  it needs its own plan scoped as a build/release change, with its own
  risk review.

## Frozen scope — the entire v2.4.3 content scope, no more

1. Hardcoded version-pin literals — add a comment/note at each site
   pointing at the single source of truth, and (if in scope after
   discussion below) a release-time grep check candidate for a future
   validator; this plan's own scope is the prose/comment fix only, not a
   new validator.
2. Misdirected metadata link — both occurrences repointed to the
   canonical slug.
3. Off-palette `#CCCCCC` in `governance-loop.dot` and
   `adoption-exit-flow.dot` — replaced with a palette-compliant color;
   grey-semantic-overload cross-checked against `palette.md` and resolved
   or explicitly accepted with reasoning recorded.
4. Level-2 naming inconsistency — one canonical name chosen (recommend
   keeping the rule's "Schema-defined" as canonical, since it is the
   more precise term per `implementation-honesty-rule.md`'s own
   generic-equivalent table, and repointing the four public-doc
   occurrences), with `maturity-vocabulary.md` as the single
   source of truth other pages reference rather than restate.
5. `bgcolor=white` tension — resolved one direction, not merely
   re-flagged: either documented as an accepted, intentional
   light-background assumption in `palette.md` itself, or made
   background-agnostic across the 7 sources. Direction decided during
   implementation planning, not left open at Gate 2.
6. `<figure>`/`<figcaption>` semantics and non-title alt text — added
   across the figure embeds already touched by this plan's other items,
   at minimum; a full sitewide pass is a stretch goal, not a commitment
   (see non-goals).

**Nothing else is in scope.** The open CLI-onboarding-pointer question
above requires an explicit decision to enter scope; it is not implicitly
included. Issue #53 and the `seq` deprecation decision are out of scope
per "Origin and scope boundary" above.

## Non-goals

- No sitewide `<figure>`/`<figcaption>` retrofit — only the embeds this
  plan's other items already touch, plus any the fact-checker/rendered-
  evidence pass flags as trivial to include at the same time.
- No new release-time validator implementation for version-pin drift —
  this plan documents the single source of truth; building an automated
  check is separately-scoped future work if wanted.
- No macOS/darwin build target (see "Explicitly out of scope" above).
- No CI-latency work (#53) and no `seq` deprecation decision.
- No change to any schema, role definition, tool/CLI surface, or
  adopter-facing contract.

## Implementation MR structure

One implementation MR, six named commits (one per frozen-scope item
above), each independently reviewable:

1. Version-pin single-source-of-truth note.
2. Metadata-link fix (2 files).
3. Off-palette color fix + grey-overload resolution (2+ figure sources).
4. Level-2 naming canonicalization (4 public-doc files +
   `maturity-vocabulary.md` as source of truth).
5. `bgcolor=white` tension resolution (7 figure sources, or
   `palette.md` alone if the accepted-assumption direction is chosen).
6. Figure `<figure>`/`<figcaption>` + alt-text pass on already-touched
   embeds.

Stop condition per commit: if fact-checker review (before Gate 2) finds a
factual defect in any commit's text that isn't a wording-level fix, that
commit is reverted and re-planned rather than patched under review
pressure — same discipline as v2.4.2's implementation MR structure.

Release packaging (`release/v2.4.3` branch, VERSION bump, CHANGELOG)
remains a later step after this MR passes Gate 2, per
`release-via-merge-request-rule` — no direct commits to `main`, no
tagging before merge, no `release/v2.4.3` branch before this plan is
Gate 1 approved.

## Patch-version justification

Every item in the frozen scope corrects a documentation/figure-quality
defect (stale link, off-palette color, naming drift, accessibility gap,
unresolved tension) on already-shipped public surfaces. None add a new
schema field, role, tool, CLI flag, MCP surface, or adopter-facing
contract — same character as v2.4.2, justifying **patch** version
treatment.

## Fact-checker consultation

**Trigger:** after the implementation MR exists, before Gate 2.

**Scope:**

- the version-pin single-source-of-truth wording (item 1) — confirm it
  doesn't overstate what's automated vs. manual;
- the Level-2 naming canonicalization (item 4) — confirm the chosen
  canonical name and its cross-references are accurate against
  `implementation-honesty-rule.md` and `maturity-vocabulary.md` as they
  exist at implementation time, not as described in this plan;
- the `bgcolor=white` resolution wording (item 5), whichever direction is
  chosen — confirm it doesn't assert a false universal claim about
  renderer/theme support.

**Not in scope for fact-checker:** the metadata-link fix (mechanical,
verifiable by clicking the link) and the figcaption/alt-text pass (an
accessibility markup change, not a factual claim).
